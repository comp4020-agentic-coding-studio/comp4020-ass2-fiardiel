## The brief

> Take a continuity failure the franchise never fixed, and write the repair.

There are four repairs: the retcon, the soft reboot, the multiverse and the
recast. Each one fixes something by breaking something else. When _Thor:
Ragnarok_ (2017) declared the gauntlet in Odin's vault a fake, it fixed a
contradiction with _Avengers: Infinity War_ and cost _Thor_ (2011) nothing but a
prop. Most repairs are not that cheap. This proposal is marked mostly on
whether you know what yours costs.

The failure can be the one from your Continuity Audit. It must still be broken.
If the franchise has repaired it on screen, choose another.

## What you submit

A proposal of no more than 1,500 words, as a PDF, written as a memo to the
franchise's head of production. It covers, in order:

1. **The failure.** Where it is, what class it is, and why it matters to the
   people who remember it.
2. **The repair.** What happens on screen, and in which title, specific enough
   that a writer could draft the scene from it.
3. **The cost.** What the repair breaks: the scenes that stop meaning what they
   meant, and the viewers who lose them.
4. **The alternatives.** Why not the other three repairs.
