## The brief

> Brief the seminar on one decision a franchise made about its continuity, and
> the option it passed over.

Every continuity strategy is a choice between options that were all available on
the day. Marvel could have recast T'Challa for _Black Panther: Wakanda Forever_
(2022). It chose not to. The Arrowverse could have kept its shows on separate
Earths. It merged them in _Crisis on Infinite Earths_ (2019–20). A briefing takes
one decision like these, rebuilds the alternative, and argues which was better.

Then it says better for whom. A decision can suit the studio's release schedule
and fail the viewer who watched every episode. Say which of them you are judging
it for. Most briefings skip this, and it is the part that is marked hardest.

Both examples above are worked through in seminars, so choose another.

## What you submit
