## The brief

> Build a continuity bible for a small original universe, good enough that a
> stranger can write an episode into it without contradicting anything.

A continuity bible is the document a writers' room checks before it breaks a
story. Week 2 covers what goes in one. Here you write your own, for a universe
you invent, and find out whether it works by handing it to someone who has never
heard of it.

Keep the universe small: three recurring characters, one location, and a
history short enough to read in one sitting. Scope is the most common way a
bible fails. Whatever it leaves out is where the stranger will contradict it,
and a bigger universe leaves more out.

## The stranger test

Bibles are swapped in the week 11 seminar. Your stranger writes a one-page
outline for a new episode from nothing but your bible, and brings it to the week
12 studio. Together you find every place the outline contradicts your universe.
Each one is a gap in the bible, not a mistake by the stranger. Log it, and
repair the bible before you submit.

## What you submit

One PDF in three parts:

1. **The bible**, no more than 4,000 words: a timeline, a character register,
   the rules of the world, and a canon policy that says what counts as canon and
   who decides.
2. **The stranger-test report**, no more than 800 words: each contradiction in
   the outline, the gap that caused it, and the repair you made. Attach the
   outline itself, unedited.
3. **The canon policy note**, no more than 600 words: why your policy, argued
   against one that a real franchise uses instead.
