## The technique

Paracanon is work that sits beside a continuity without being clearly in it or
out of it. A canon policy is the rule that decides, and says who gets to decide.
Without one, the audience decides, and it doesn't agree with itself.

## The cases

_The LEGO Ninjago Movie_ (2017) retells _Ninjago_ with different origins and a
different city. It never mentions the series, and the series never mentions it.
That is paracanon handled cleanly: two continuities, and nobody asks which is
true.

Marvel's Netflix series, from _Daredevil_ (2015) on, are the opposite case. They
were made under the Marvel name and never acknowledged by the films. Then
Charlie Cox appeared as Matt Murdock in _Spider-Man: No Way Home_ (2021), and
Vincent D'Onofrio as the Kingpin in _Hawkeye_ (2021). Neither appearance said
whether the Netflix seasons had happened.

## In the seminar

Write the canon policy Marvel could have published for the Netflix series in
2015, and test it against what happened next. Then revise the draft for your
Capstone universe. The Capstone asks you to argue your policy against one a
real franchise uses, so note which one you are arguing with.
