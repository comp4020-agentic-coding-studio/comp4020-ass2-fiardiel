## The technique

Spin-off drift is the distance between what a spin-off inherits and what it
becomes. Some drift is the point: a spin-off exists to do what its parent can't.
The engineering question is which rules it may leave behind, and who decided.

## The case: Beebo

_DC's Legends of Tomorrow_ launched in 2016 out of _Arrow_ and _The Flash_, as a
team of minor heroes chasing an immortal villain through time. Its third season
ends, in 2018, with a giant blue toy called Beebo fighting a demon. Nothing in
the first season forbids it, and nothing in _Arrow_ allows it. The show found
its audience in exactly that gap.

## In the seminar

We diagnose the drift the way the Retcon Proposal's first section does: find
where it starts, and classify it. Is it drift, or a new continuity wearing the
old one's cast? Then we write the bible rule that would have allowed Beebo on
purpose.
