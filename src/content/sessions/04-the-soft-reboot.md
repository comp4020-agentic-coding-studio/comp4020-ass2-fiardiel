## The technique

A hard reboot starts again and says so. A soft reboot keeps enough of the old
continuity to keep its audience, and stops honouring the parts that got in the
way. It never announces which parts those are. The audience finds out one
missing reference at a time.

## The case: the Merge

_Ninjago: Dragons Rising_ (2023) follows fifteen seasons of _Ninjago_. It opens
with the Merge, an event that fuses the realms and scatters the ninja. Some of
the cast carries over. A new lead, Arin, arrives. The old continuity still
happened, officially. The show is built so that a new viewer never needs it.

## In the seminar

The soft reboot sits between the retcon, which changes a meaning, and the
multiverse, which changes the universe. We map what _Dragons Rising_ kept and
what it dropped, and rank the drops by how soon a returning viewer would notice.
Then the Retcon Proposal's question: what did it cost, and who paid?
