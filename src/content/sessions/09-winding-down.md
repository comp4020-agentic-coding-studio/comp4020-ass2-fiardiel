## The technique

Winding down is ending a continuity on purpose: deciding which threads to close
before the last episode, and which to leave open. What a long-running story owes
the people who remember it is clearest at the end, when it can no longer pay
them later.

## The case: two endings

_Arrow_ knew its eighth season would be its last, and built that short season
around _Crisis_. It ends with Oliver Queen's funeral. _DC's Legends of Tomorrow_
ended its seventh season in 2022 with its team under arrest, expecting an
eighth. The network cancelled it. The arrest is still the last thing that
happened to them.

## In the seminar

We evaluate both endings against the alternatives each had. _Arrow_ could have
ended without the funeral. _Legends_ could have ended its season as if it might
be the last. Say which alternative was better, and who for.
