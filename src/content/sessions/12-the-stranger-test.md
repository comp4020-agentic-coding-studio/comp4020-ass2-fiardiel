## The technique

The stranger test checks a bible by handing it to someone who has never heard of
the universe and asking them to write into it. Whatever they get wrong, the
bible failed to say. Every bible sits this test the day a new writer joins the
room, whether anyone calls it a test or not.

## In the seminar

Sit with your stranger and go through their outline against your bible, one
scene at a time. Each contradiction goes in the log, with the gap in the bible
that caused it. Then swap roles. The repairs are yours to make before the
Capstone is due.
