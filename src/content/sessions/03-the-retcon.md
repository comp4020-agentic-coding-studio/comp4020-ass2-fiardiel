## The technique

A retcon, short for retroactive continuity, adds new information that changes
what an earlier scene meant. The earlier scene stays exactly as it was. Only its
meaning moves.

## The case: the fake gauntlet

In _Thor_ (2011), an Infinity Gauntlet sits in Odin's vault. In the mid-credits
scene of _Avengers: Age of Ultron_ (2015), Thanos puts one on. Two gauntlets is
a contradiction. In _Thor: Ragnarok_ (2017), Hela knocks the one in the vault
over and calls it a fake. One line fixes it, and nothing in _Thor_ has to mean
anything different except a prop.

_Skybound_ (2016), the sixth season of _Ninjago_, ends with a wish that undoes
the season's events. That is a retcon too. It cost the season.

## In the seminar

We price the two. For each retcon you bring: what the new information changed,
what it cost, and who paid. The Retcon Proposal is marked mostly on cost, so
this is the week to learn to count it.
