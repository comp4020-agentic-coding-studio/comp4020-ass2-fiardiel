## The technique

A recast replaces the actor and keeps the character. There are four options:
say nothing, acknowledge it and move on, explain the new face inside the story,
or retire the character with the actor.

## The cases

- **Say nothing.** Edward Norton plays Bruce Banner in _The Incredible Hulk_
  (2008). Mark Ruffalo plays him from _The Avengers_ (2012). No one on screen
  mentions it.
- **Acknowledge it.** Terrence Howard plays James Rhodes in _Iron Man_ (2008).
  Don Cheadle takes over in _Iron Man 2_ (2010), and his first scene tells the
  audience to deal with it.
- **Explain it.** When Ruby Rose left _Batwoman_ after its first season, the
  show gave the suit to a new character, Ryan Wilder. Kate Kane came back later
  with a new face and a new actor, Wallis Day, and the plot explained the face.
- **Refuse.** After Chadwick Boseman died in 2020, _Black Panther: Wakanda
  Forever_ (2022) did not recast T'Challa. The film is about his death.

## In the seminar

Each option spends something different. Saying nothing spends the audience's
goodwill. Explaining it spends plot. Refusing spends the character. Choose a
recast you think took the wrong option, and argue the cost of the right one.
