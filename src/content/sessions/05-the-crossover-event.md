## The technique

A crossover event runs one story across several series. Done as a stunt, it is
a ratings week. Done as engineering, it is the one moment a franchise can change
every continuity at once, because every show is in the room.

## The case: Crisis on Infinite Earths

The pilot of _The Flash_ (2014) shows a newspaper from the future, dated 2024:
"Flash missing, vanishes in crisis". The Arrowverse carried that promise for
five years, then paid it off early. _Crisis on Infinite Earths_ ran across five
series in December 2019 and January 2020. It merged the separate Earths of
_Supergirl_ and _Black Lightning_ into one, Earth-Prime. It also gave _Arrow_,
which was ending, the death of Oliver Queen.
