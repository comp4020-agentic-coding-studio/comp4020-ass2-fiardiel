## The technique

A continuity bible is the reference a writers' room checks before it commits to
a story. It holds four things: a timeline, a register of who is who, the rules of
the world, and a canon policy that says what counts. It is the record from week
1, written down before the audience has to write it for you.

## The case: eight years later

_Spider-Man: Homecoming_ (2017) opens in the wreckage of the Battle of New York
from _The Avengers_ (2012), then cuts forward with a title card: "Eight years
later". The rest of the film follows straight on from Peter Parker's trip to
Berlin in _Captain America: Civil War_ (2016). Both cannot be true. One line in
a bible, the year each film is set, would have caught it before the card was
made.

## In the seminar

We build the bible entry _Homecoming_ needed: Peter Parker's timeline, from the
facts you bring. Then you start your own. The Capstone bible begins here, so the
universe you pitch today is the one a stranger will try to write into at the
end of semester. Continuity Briefing rosters are set before you leave.
