## The technique

Every continuity has a rate: how much new canon it asks its audience to keep up
with each year. Franchise fatigue is what happens past that rate. Viewers stop
keeping the record, and a story that depends on the record stops working for
them.

## The case: The Marvels

_The Marvels_ (2023) needed its audience to have watched two Disney+ series,
_WandaVision_ and _Ms. Marvel_, to know two of its three leads. It became the
lowest-grossing film in the MCU. Disney then said it would release fewer Marvel
films and series each year.

## In the seminar

We lay the slate you bring on one timeline and find where the rate broke. Was it
the number of releases, or how much each one depended on the others? Briefings
run as usual. Then Capstone bibles are swapped for the stranger test: you leave
with someone else's universe and a week to write an episode into it.
