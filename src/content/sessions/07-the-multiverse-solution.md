## The technique

The multiverse solution declares a second universe, so that a contradiction
stops being a contradiction and becomes two Earths. It can absorb any failure.
That is the problem with it.

## The case: three Spider-Men

_Spider-Man: No Way Home_ (2021) brings back Tobey Maguire's and Andrew
Garfield's Spider-Men, from five films made outside the MCU between 2002 and
2014, as visitors from other universes. Films that could never be reconciled
with the MCU are reconciled in one move: they happened, somewhere else.
_Deadpool & Wolverine_ (2024) does the same for the Fox _X-Men_ films. The
Arrowverse ran on this solution for years before _Crisis_ collapsed it.

## In the seminar

We run the Retcon Proposal's cost test on the multiverse. Once any contradiction
can be moved to another Earth, what stops meaning anything? Deaths usually go
first. Bring the one you think goes next.
