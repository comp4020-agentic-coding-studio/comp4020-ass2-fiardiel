Every repair in this course fixes one thing by breaking another. This lecture
sets the four side by side, so the seminars that follow can take them one at a
time.

| Repair      | What it changes                                  | Seminar |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| Retcon      | what an earlier scene meant                      | week 3  |
| Soft reboot | which parts of the continuity are still honoured | week 4  |
| Multiverse  | which universe a fact belongs to                 | week 7  |
| Recast      | the face, not the character                      | week 8  |

## Outline

- **What each repair touches**, and what it leaves alone.
- **Counting the cost.** The scenes that stop working, and the viewers who
  remember them.
- **Choosing.** Why the Retcon Proposal asks you to reject the other three, and
  why the cost carries the most marks.
