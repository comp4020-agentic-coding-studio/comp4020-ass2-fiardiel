This lecture is given from the slides. The case is _Crisis on Infinite Earths_;
the method is the one every Continuity Briefing follows.

## Outline

- **A promise with a date on it.** The newspaper in the pilot of _The Flash_.
- **The decision, and the option it passed over.** Merging the Earths, or
  keeping them apart.
- **Pricing both, and saying for whom.** The studio, the viewer who watches
  every show, and the viewer who watches one.
- **The method.** Five steps, in the order a Briefing takes them.
