A bible is only as good as the next writer's ability to use it. This lecture
covers how to build one that a stranger can, which is what the Capstone tests.

## Outline

- **The four parts.** A timeline, a character register, the rules of the world,
  and a canon policy.
- **Rules, not history.** A list of events tells a writer what happened. A rule
  tells them what can't happen next. Bibles need both, and usually lack the
  second.
- **Findability.** A fact the writer cannot find in a minute is not in the
  bible.
- **Gaps on purpose.** What to leave undecided, and how to mark it so no one
  mistakes silence for permission.
- **The stranger test.** Why the Capstone ends with one, and how to run a small
  one on yourself before week 11.
