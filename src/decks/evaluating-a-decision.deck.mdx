---

## A promise with a date on it

The pilot of _The Flash_, 2014: a newspaper from the future.

**"Flash missing, vanishes in crisis"**, dated 2024.

```notes
The Arrowverse carried this for five years. Everyone watching knew the date.
```

---

## Before _Crisis_

- _Arrow_, _The Flash_, _Legends_, _Batwoman_: one Earth
- _Supergirl_: another
- _Black Lightning_: another

Every crossover needed a way through.

---

## The decision

December 2019 to January 2020. Five series, one event.

The Earths merge into one: **Earth-Prime**.

---

## The option it passed over

Pay off the promise. **Keep the Earths apart.**

```notes
Rebuild the alternative from what was available on the day. The multiverse had run for years, and nothing on screen forced the merge.
```

---

## Price both

| Option     | Keeps                   | Costs                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Merge      | cheap crossovers        | _Supergirl_'s own Earth      |
| Keep apart | each show's own history | a device for every crossover |

---

## For whom?

- the studio, planning next season's crossovers
- the viewer who watches every show
- the viewer who only watches _Supergirl_

A decision can be right for one and wrong for the others.
